Frequently asked questions

Before you take the next step.

What does RexaEdge currently support? ＋

RexaEdge provides an end-to-end guided operations platform for SAP Bill of Materials workflows: CS01 production creation, CS02 matrix cloning, CS03 component hierarchy inspection, pre-flight data dictionary validation, and controlled deletion with complete audit logging across multi-plant environments.

Does RexaEdge replace SAP? ＋

No. RexaEdge is a modern, high-speed operational intelligence layer that integrates directly into your existing SAP S/4HANA or ECC instance. All master records, business rules, and transaction commits remain securely stored and executed in your enterprise SAP database.

Will it work with our SAP system? ＋

Yes. RexaEdge supports SAP S/4HANA (on-premise and private cloud) as well as SAP ECC 6.0 (EHP7/EHP8). It establishes secure bi-directional communication via standard RFC with TLS 1.3 encryption and SAP SNC credentials, requiring zero core ABAP modifications.

How can we evaluate RexaEdge? ＋

You can schedule a personalized 1-on-1 walkthrough with an SAP solution specialist, explore our interactive sandbox environment, or review architecture blueprints for your specific plant topology and authorization matrix.
